# Cybersecurity Simulation

Welcome to **Cybersecurity Simulation**, a personal project by **Tomisin Farinola** — a passionate law and technology enthusiast exploring the intersection between **cybersecurity**, **law**, and **digital governance**.

---

##  About the Project

This repository simulates cybersecurity scenarios that demonstrate how individuals and organizations can **identify**, **prevent**, and **respond** to cyber threats.

It’s designed to strengthen both **technical** and **legal understanding** of cybersecurity issues — from penetration testing and digital forensics to compliance with data protection laws like the **NDPR**, **Cybercrime Act**, and **GDPR**.

---

## Objectives

- Demonstrate how cyberattacks occur and how to defend against them.  
- Examine the **legal implications** of cybersecurity breaches.  
- Promote awareness of cybersecurity policies and data protection.  
- Develop analytical and technical skills in real-world security environments.  

---

## 💻 Featured Projects

### 1. 🕵️‍♂️ Phishing Attack Simulation  
Simulated an email phishing campaign to analyze how unsuspecting users respond to social engineering attacks.  
- Tools: Social-Engineer Toolkit (SET), Gmail API  
- Focus: Awareness, ethical hacking, user education  
- Legal Insight: Data protection and consent under NDPR  

---

### 2. 🔒 Network Vulnerability Assessment  
Performed a mock penetration test on a local virtual network to identify open ports, weak credentials, and outdated services.  
- Tools: Nmap, Wireshark, Metasploit  
- Focus: Risk assessment, network security hardening  
- Legal Insight: Responsible disclosure and computer misuse laws  

---

### 3. 🧾 Digital Forensics Investigation  
Simulated a data breach scenario where log analysis and metadata tracing were used to identify the intruder.  
- Tools: Autopsy, FTK Imager, Volatility  
- Focus: Evidence preservation, forensic reporting  
- Legal Insight: Admissibility of electronic evidence in Nigeria (Section 84, Evidence Act 2011)  

---

### 4. ⚖️ Cyber Law Compliance Tracker  
Developed a basic policy checklist for Nigerian SMEs to comply with the **NDPR** and **Cybercrime Act** using Python scripts.  
- Tools: Python, Pandas, Excel  
- Focus: Regulatory compliance, automation  
- Legal Insight: Data privacy, consent management, and accountability principles
